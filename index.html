How to Play

You explore your opponent's (AI's) hidden field. Find the real Treasure and carry it out through the Exit before the AI does. You take turns, like in chess.

Swamp — harmless while exploring; once the Exit has appeared, it starts skipping your next turn (and brightens so you can tell).
🕳️
Hole — teleports you to another Hole and ends your turn immediately. The cycle is always 1→2→3→4→1, but which physical cell has which number is unknown. You can tag a Hole cell with your own personal label (button on the cell) to track the order yourself.
🪤
Trap — you lose any Treasure(s) you're carrying and your ability to blow up walls.
💣
Armory — grants Bombs: lets you learn a wall's type and blow it up.
💰
Treasure — there are two on the opponent's field: real and fake. You won't know which one you have until you try to leave.
Puzzles: after a Hole you lose your orientation — the board switches to a compact puzzle layout: your known map shrinks into one corner and new cells are drawn as a separate fragment "as you walked it", not on the shared map. Once you work out where it really is, tap "?" on the map, then the fragment cell, to anchor it.

What the cells do
Land — open ground, no effect.
Swamp — harmless while exploring; skips your next turn once the Exit has appeared.
Hole — teleports you along the cycle; disorients you.
Trap — strips any Treasure and Bombs you're carrying.
Armory — grants Bombs, for blowing up destructible walls.
Treasure — pick it up (real or fake — you won't know which).
River (R#) — ride it toward the Mouth; fight it and you're swept back.
Mouth (M) — the river's end; safe to stand on.
